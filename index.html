GEMFALL Privacy Policy

App: GEMFALL for Android (package com.cmurray.gemfall)

Developer: C.Murray

Contact: gemfall105@gmail.com

Effective date: 26 November 2026

Summary

GEMFALL is a cave-digging puzzle game for one or two players. You do not need an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Your progress is saved on your device.

The free Android version shows ads from Google AdMob and offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play, managed with RevenueCat. Those two services receive some data from your device, as described below. Online co-op connects your device directly to your partner's device.

We (the developer) do not run any server for the game, we do not receive your game data, and we do not sell personal data.

1. Data stored on your device

The game stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • progress: cleared caves, stars, best scores and times, high-score tables and play statistics;
  • the in-game wallet: Shards (the in-game currency), rewind batteries, unlocked hints and solutions, robot skins, the daily login streak and ad pacing counters (for example, when an ad was last shown);
  • settings, such as volume, controls, lighting quality, co-op rules and, if you enter one, the address of a room-code server for online co-op.

This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Android's own backup service may include app data in your device backup if you have it turned on; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show:

  • full-screen (interstitial) ads between caves, at most one every few minutes and never during a cave or in co-op;
  • rewarded ads, only when you tap an offer (for example "+30 shards").

What the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects (from Google's Play data disclosure for the SDK):

  • device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (not your precise location);
  • product interactions with ads and the app (for example app launches, taps and ad views);
  • diagnostic information about app and SDK performance (for example launch time and hang rate).

Google uses this data to show, measure and limit ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud, and it may share it with its ad partners as described in Google's policies. The data is encrypted in transit.

Consent and choices.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and in other places where the law requires it, the app shows Google's consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. You can choose whether ads may be personalised. Where this applies, "Ad privacy options" in the game's Settings lets you review or change your choice at any time.
  • Some US states give you the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. Where Google's US state privacy message is enabled, it is shown in the same way.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID on your phone (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your phone).
  • Ads are requested as not child-directed, because the game is meant for players aged 13 and over (see section 6).

If you buy Remove Ads, the app stops showing interstitial ads. Rewarded ads are still offered, but only play if you choose them.

More information: "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services", https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's Privacy Policy, https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing and RevenueCat)

  • Purchases (Remove Ads and Shard packs) are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or other payment details. Google Play's handling of your payment is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • The app uses RevenueCat to check and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives your purchase history for this app (which product, the purchase token and transaction details, time and price), your device's locale and currency code, and an anonymous app user ID that RevenueCat creates for this installation. We do not link this ID to your name, email or any other account.
  • This data is used to give you what you bought, to let "Restore purchases" work (for example after reinstalling) and for basic sales statistics. It is encrypted in transit.
  • RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

4. Online co-op

Online co-op is optional. When you use it:

  • Your device connects directly to your partner's device (WebRTC, peer to peer). The connection is encrypted.
  • To make the connection, the game uses a public STUN server run by Google (stun.l.google.com). It sees your device's public IP address so that it can tell your device that address; no game data passes through it.
  • The invite and answer codes that you copy and send to your partner contain the network addresses (IP addresses and ports) of your device, so that the two devices can find each other. You choose how to send them (for example by a chat app), and anyone you send a code to can see those addresses.
  • Your partner's device receives your IP address (needed for any direct connection) and the game messages: your moves, pause and restart requests, the chosen cave and co-op rules, connection checks and your co-op rewind allowance. There is no text or voice chat, and no names, profiles or account details are sent. The robots are always called Pip and Dot.
  • Room codes (optional). If you enter a room-code server address in Settings, the game sends the connection codes through that server instead of copy and paste. The game ships with no server set, and we do not run one. If you use a server, it is run by whoever gave you its address, and that person can see the connection codes and your IP address.

We do not record or store online games.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, messages, audio or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use any analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.
  • The app's Android permissions are: internet access, vibration (for touch feedback), Google Play Billing and the advertising ID permission used by AdMob.

6. Children

GEMFALL is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are requested as not child-directed. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and data is held by one of our providers, contact us at gemfall105@gmail.com and we will help remove it (see section 7).

7. Keeping and deleting data

  • On your device: kept until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play and RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide and restore your purchases and to meet legal and tax obligations. To ask us to delete the RevenueCat record for your purchases, email gemfall105@gmail.com with the order number from your Google Play receipt (Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Budget & history). Deleting it means "Restore purchases" may no longer restore Remove Ads.
  • Advertising data: handled by Google under its policies. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID disconnects past ad data from your device.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or US state privacy laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, most requests are handled through the controls above or by the providers (Google, RevenueCat). Contact us at gemfall105@gmail.com and we will help.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and the effective date above, and say so in the app's release notes.
